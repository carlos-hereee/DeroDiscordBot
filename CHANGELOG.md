# Changelog

All notable changes to the ROK Commander bot are documented in this file.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

This file starts at v1.4.0. Earlier releases predate the changelog convention; the public-facing summaries for those releases live in the historical `embedContent.featureAnnouncement` blocks rendered to each guild via the boot-time announcement system.

## [Unreleased]

⚠️ **This file skips from 1.4.0 to here.** Releases 1.5.x and 1.6.x shipped without changelog entries; `package.json` is at 1.6.1. That gap is not filled below, it is only noted so a reader does not mistake this entry for the whole of what changed since April.

### Added

* **Game rooms.** A two-player or cooperative match now plays in its own Discord channel under a second managed category, 🎮 ARCADE GAMES. The Challenge Board in `🕹️games` is where a member starts a challenge, which posts to `⚔️challenges`; the host chooses **Invite only** or **Open to all**, gathers who is in, and opens the room. The room's full move list is kept as an append-only transcript that outlives the channel. Each room's channel name starts with its game's emoji. When a match ends, **Rematch** starts the next one in the same channel with the same players, for every game, solo blackjack and solo hangman included: the finished match keeps its transcript and releases the channel to the new one. A room nobody acts in for 30 minutes is closed as abandoned, so it never holds its host's one room; a Monopoly match (its 24 hour seat clock rules it) and a hangman round in play are exempt.
* **Hangman with a host-authored word.** The host writes the word behind a modal with a required hint and up to three extra hints, earned by the room at 2, 4 and 5 wrong guesses, or picks one of ten categories and Dero draws a word with its hint from a 300 word bank (30 a category). The hint is not checked against the word. Once the word is set the room's button becomes **Guess a letter**, and every guess posts the gallows card. Every letter played is its own attributed message. The host cannot guess their own word, and the word appears in exactly one message, the result card. Hangman has no clock: a round in play waits until it ends or its host presses **Give up**. Solo hangman draws from the shelf a member picks in `🕹️games` and shows the word's hint.
* **`GET /api/game-rooms`** for the dashboard, behind the existing signature gate. Returns finished matches newest first, excluding the hidden word and the full move list.
* **A room archive sweep** on its own 5 minute cron. A finished room stays readable for four hours, or clears immediately when the host presses **Archive now**.
* **Dero's voice and the Listen button.** An admin turns voice on with `/dero-config enable`. Dero speaks as George, the built in ElevenLabs voice, so there is no voice id to set. A **Listen** button on welcomes, and on fortunes where the member did not type a question, reads the post aloud as a private mp3, or plays it in the voice call when the member pressing it is in the same call as Dero. Voice ships OFF in every server, and every paid speech call is checked first against a monthly budget per server ($5 by default, $0 refuses everything).
* **`/dero tell`.** A member on the server's task list (the server owner, or anyone holding a role an admin added) gives Dero a short task in plain words, for example posting a message in an allowed channel. Dero's brain only proposes; the bot checks the member again, posts only in allowed channels, and never pings anyone.
* **Voice calls: `/dero join`, `/dero leave` and `/dero status`.** Dero joins an allowed voice channel and posts, on every join, that it is listening only to members on its task list, that their speech is sent to ElevenLabs for speech to text, and that the call is not recorded by Dero. It answers aloud, keeps no audio and no transcript, and leaves 2 minutes after the last task list member does, or at once when it is moved, kicked, its channel is deleted, or voice is switched off.
* **Hold'em by request, with Dero as the dealer.** Asked for a table, Dero shows a rules card with the standard rules or the asker's own seats, stack and big blind. The asker picks a public or an invite only table and a real room opens; Dero posts the hand start and the result there and, for a table asked for in a call, speaks the deal, the streets and the result. Hole cards are never spoken or posted, and chips exist only for that table.
* **Hold'em rooms on a live clock.** Each decision has 60 seconds on a live countdown that starts when the player is prompted. The next hand deals itself 10 seconds after a hand ends while two seats are in, and the host can still deal sooner. A seat that times out twice in a row sits out (posting its blinds and folding each hand) until it presses **I'm back**. Any seat can **Leave table**, which folds a live hand and takes the seat out like a bust, its chips gone with it since chips exist only for that table, and the host can **End table**, which finishes the table with its standings and pays no placement XP.
* **Monopoly, for 2 to 6 players.** Started from the Challenge Board in `🕹️games` or with `/mini-game monopoly`, and played in its own room, a turn at a time, across as many days as the table likes. The full ruleset on Dero's own 40 square board: buying, rent, 16 Chance and 16 Community Chest cards, jail with three ways out (bail, a Get Out of Jail Free card, or doubles), houses and hotels built evenly, private trades of lots, chips and jail cards, mortgages, and selling buildings back to the bank. The host chooses at the challenge card whether a lot a player passes on goes to a 60 second sealed bid auction. A player short of chips for a debt they could cover by mortgaging or selling is held until they raise it and press **Pay**; one who could not cover it is bankrupt at once. A seat that goes 24 hours on its turn without a move is forfeited, and a debtor who never pays is bankrupt to whoever they owed. The board is a picture of the board art with tokens, houses and hotels when the image library loads, and a text map otherwise. The last seat standing wins, and the result shows in `/account`.
* **Dero as the Monopoly voice referee.** A second toggle on the Monopoly challenge card. With it on, and while Dero is in a voice call that a player still in the match is in, Dero speaks the key moments of each move: whose turn it is, doubles rerolls, purchases, rents of $200 or more, going to jail and getting out, the card drawn (its title only), auctions opening and closing, a held debt, a bankruptcy, the winner, and the idle forfeits, with names in place of mentions. It never joins a call on its own, needs voice switched on with `/dero-config`, and every clip it renders counts against the same monthly speech budget. Nothing is posted in text, since the match's own trail already shows every move.
* **`/dero-config`** for administrators: status, enable, disable, listen, cap, the allowlists of roles, text channels and voice channels, and the `arcade` group (`monthly-post`, `reach`).
* **Voice and tasks run through Dero's brain service.** `/dero tell`, spoken answers in a call and new table requests are answered by the brain over a signed link; while the link is not connected, Dero tells the member its brain is offline and nothing runs. Listen, joining a call and the narration of a table already open do not use the brain.
* **Drawn cards in place of text.** `/account`, milestone level ups (a looping picture on a prime level), the levels board, an achievement unlock, a role reward unlock, the hangman gallows and end card, the battleship boards (the private fleet included), the blackjack table on every turn (the dealer's hole card face down) and its result, the Hold'em table after every action at every table and the showdown with its stack bars and action strip, the Monopoly trade, auction and winner cards, Dero's voice presence in a call, the welcome, the fortune scene and the challenge card (led by the game's logo, and for battleship the Admiral, where that art exists, above the players' faces) now post as rendered images, drawn with the owner's arcade art and Dero's v2 poses. The `#introductions` welcome shows Dero's v2 welcome pose from the bot's own files. While a card draws, blackjack, battleship, Monopoly and Hold'em show "Dero is drawing the table" and hold their clocks, so a draw never costs a turn. Every card falls back to the text embed it replaced when the image library does not load or a draw fails, and nothing a member sees turns into an error. A card that carries private terms (a trade offer, a sealed bid, a private fleet) is never cached. Rendered cards are cached in memory and, when `DISCORD_BOT_S3_ENABLED` is on with the `DISCORD_BOT_AWS_*` variables set, in S3 under `discord-bot/renders/`.
* **A weekly digest by DM.** Every Monday at 9 am America/Chicago, each server's 25 most present members of the week get a private card of their week, 1.5 seconds apart. A server with leveling off or level up announcements switched off sends none. `WEEKLY_DIGEST_RUN_ON_BOOT=1` runs one pass at boot, for an operator's hand check.
* **An optional soak sampler** for operators: with `DERO_VOICE_SAMPLER` set to `1`, one audit row a minute while a call is open, numbers only (memory, calls, rejoins, queue depth, turns, this month's speech spend, latency median). Off by default.
* **`/render-analytics top10` for the platform owner only** (`CREATOR_DISCORD_ID`, ephemeral, optional `surface` and `period`: this month, last month or all time). Every rendered card lookup is counted per UTC month as a memory hit, an S3 hit or a draw, and every avatar lookup per size as a memory hit or a download, with no user, server or avatar identity stored. It shows per surface totals with hit rates and sizing hints, the memory tier, and the 10 most served cards. Counting never waits on the database and never affects a render; `RENDER_ANALYTICS_ENABLED=false` turns it off.
* **A weekly render analytics digest for the platform owner.** Every Monday at 9:15 am America/Chicago the owner gets a DM with this month's render analytics so far, the same report `/render-analytics top10` shows. It is skipped, with one log line, when `CREATOR_DISCORD_ID` is unset or `RENDER_ANALYTICS_ENABLED` is off.
* **`GET /api/platform/render-analytics?period=`** (this month, last month or all time) answers the same report as JSON for Dero's Castle. It sits behind the signature gate with no `guildId`, because the rows belong to no server, and only the platform server calls it.
* **Arcade ratings and `/rating`.** Finished Texas hold em (10 or more dealt hands), Battleship and Monopoly matches move each player's rating (OpenSkill, shown as `1000 + 40 x (mu minus 3 sigma)`), and coin flip, dice roll and rock paper scissors feed a monthly luck board instead. `/rating me [game]` shows a member their own rating per rated game, `/rating board <game>` this server's best rated current members of that game, and `/rating luck` this server's luckiest members this month, scored by wins above an even split from 20 decided games. A rating from fewer than 10 rated games carries a `?`. Nothing is shown across servers. The command is named in the member command guide, and `📋arcade-rules` explains which games are rated and how the luck board scores.
* **A monthly arcade post.** On the 1st at 9:05 am America/Chicago each server's leaderboard channel gets last month's top three winning Hold'em hands, the top rated player of each rated game among its players, and its luckiest player, as a drawn card with a text fallback. A month with nothing to show posts nothing, and a month is claimed before it posts, so it never posts twice. `/dero-config arcade monthly-post enabled:false` turns it off for a server; it is on by default. For a test guild, `ARCADE_MONTHLY_POST_RUN_ON_BOOT=1` posts a preview of the current month so far at boot, titled "so far". It claims nothing, so the real post on the 1st still goes out and every boot with the flag set posts the preview again; unset it after the check.
* **Challenge reach and host controls.** `/dero-config arcade reach mode:` sets how far a server's challenges may reach: **Invite only** (hosts open invite only rooms; Open to all is not offered and is refused, a rematch of a public room included), **Server** (today's behavior, the default) or **Network** (stored now, and allows what Server does until network tables arrive). On an Open to all challenge the host can set a rating band for Hold'em, Battleship and Monopoly (Any, Close within 150, Tight within 75; a player with fewer than 10 rated games fits any band, and so does everyone at a table whose host has fewer) and approve each joiner from a **Requests** panel. **Remove a player** takes a member out of the challenge before it starts, or out of a Hold'em, Battleship or Monopoly match from the room's pinned opener: a Hold'em seat leaves the table, a Battleship match ends unrated, a Monopoly seat goes out through the forfeit path. A removed member cannot rejoin that room by any button or menu, their own channel permission is taken away, and the match never moves their rating. While a Hold'em tournament runs the host can **Open seats**: a joiner who passes the band and the approval takes a seat that is dealt in at the start of the next hand with the table's starting stack, and **Close seats** stops new ones.
* **`🕹️games`, the arcade's front door.** A third channel under 🎮 ARCADE GAMES lists every game on one line each and holds every way to start one: the host row (a solo blackjack table or one others may join), the solo games against Dero, a hangman shelf list for a solo round, and the Challenge Board beneath them. Existing servers get it on their next boot, once, unless auto heal is off or an admin removed the slot. `📋arcade-rules` holds the arcade's rules only (rooms, one room per host, the idle close, rematch, what playing earns, ratings), and `⚔️challenges` holds only challenge posts.
* **Solo games against Dero.** Coin flip, dice roll and rock paper scissors against Dero, played at once from the `🕹️games` buttons or from `/mini-game coin-flip`, `dice-roll` and `rps` with `against:` Dero: 3 XP for a win, 1 for a loss or a tie. They move no rating and add nothing to the luck board.
* **A game request ballot.** **Don't see your favorite game?** on `📋arcade-rules` opens a private ballot of requested games, ranked by votes with their counts. A member votes once per game (picking it again takes the vote back) and may add a game of 2 to 40 characters with their vote counted; a name already on the ballot, ignoring case, is refused and named. The server owner or an admin can also remove a game. A ballot holds at most 25 games, Discord's ceiling for one menu, and refuses a new one while full.
* **A daily XP cap.** Messages, reactions and solo games against Dero together earn a member at most 50 XP a day, the day turning at midnight America/Chicago, counted in the database so a restart does not reset it. Voice, room matches, fortunes, achievements and admin grants are not capped.
* **Buttons for commands, by audience.** The `📖command-center` guide carries Account, Levels, Achievements, Suggestion Box, Toggle pings and Fortune, each answering privately; the `🎛️admin-controls` guide carries one button per admin command, which walks the command's own subcommands and form and checks the admin gate again at every step; each voice room's text chat gets a card with `/dero`'s Join, Leave and Status. A button and its command share one handler.

### Changed

* **`/mini-game coin-flip`, `dice-roll` and `rps` no longer take an `opponent`.** With no `against`, or `against:` a member, they post a challenge card to `⚔️challenges`; `against:` Dero plays Dero on the spot. A Discord user picker can return a bot, which is why the bot used to carry a "pick a real member" refusal; the two ways into a match are now a bot-populated menu and a button, so the refusal has nothing left to refuse and was removed.
* **`/mini-game hangman` outside a room has no clock** and is refused inside one, where the room's own controls play the round.
* **Admin commands follow the `/setup` admin role.** No admin command carries Discord's Administrator default any more, because it hid the commands from the very admin role `/setup` names. Every admin command shows in every member's picker, and Dero's own check admits the server owner and the `/setup` admin role and refuses everyone else. `/setup` and `/self-destruct` stay server owner only, and `/render-analytics` platform owner only.
* **Merged commands.** `/schedule pause|resume [event]` replaces `/pause-schedule`, `/continue-schedule`, `/pause-all-schedules` and `/continue-all-schedules` (no event acts on every event); `/stream announce|soon|setup` replaces `/announce-stream`, `/go-live-soon` and `/configure-stream-schedule`; `/configure auto-heal|leaderboard-tracking|week-start` replaces `/configure-auto-heal`, `/configure-leaderboard-tracking` and `/configure-leaderboard-week-start`; `/events list|delete` replaces `/list-events` and `/delete-event` (list stays a member view, delete is admin only); `/channels rename` replaces `/rename-channel`; `/account view:levels|achievements` replaces `/levels` and `/achievements`. 19 commands remain. The owner runs `npm run build` before `npm run deploy`, so stale compiled files do not register the old names again.
* **Messages and reactions pay a flat 1 XP.** `/setup-level-system rates` no longer takes `message-min`, `message-max`, `reaction-min` or `reaction-max`, and values stored for them are ignored. Role and channel multipliers still apply on top, and the cooldowns are unchanged.
* **Battleship posts each shot once,** in the channel, with no private copy to the shooter.
* **On an existing server's next boot** the superseded `📋arcade-rules` and `⚔️challenges` intros keep their words and lose the buttons that moved to `🕹️games`, and the welcome, the guides and the rules card repost once below the old ones, because their copy changed.

### Removed

* **The old challenge handshake** (`challengeFlow`, and the duel and rps handlers built on it). It kept a match in process memory, so a Railway deploy mid-handshake lost it; a room row survives the deploy.
* **`/ping`, `/user` and the 15 command names merged above.** They leave Discord when the owner registers commands at the deploy. Four dead command files under `commands/events/` that the loader warned about are gone too.
* **`/dero-config voice`.** George is built in, and the voice copy no longer sends admins to the Suggestion Box for a voice id.

## [1.4.0]: 2026-04-27

### Fixed

* **Ancient Ruins cadence** corrected from 36h to 40h. Previously the bot's pinned next-occurrence walked earlier than the canonical ROK schedule by 4 hours every cycle, accumulating into multi-day drift over a season.
* **Altar of Darkness cadence** corrected from 84h to 86h. Same drift pattern as Ruins, 2 hours per cycle.
* **Confirmation embed strings** in `embed-content.ts` updated to match the new cadences, so the `/configure-kvk-season` preview and the post-confirm response no longer announce a cadence the bot will not actually use.
* **Season-end announcement spam.** The dedup record in `ReminderLog` is now keyed on a synthetic `season-end:<guildId>` instead of the per-event `eventId`. A guild with N expired events in a single cron tick now receives one season-end embed instead of N. Reproduces the 4/24 incident in a unit test so it cannot regress.
* **Season-end embed copy** rewritten from a transactional "kingdom stands down, run `/configure-kvk-season`" to a kingdom-voiced thank-you that acknowledges members' participation through the season. The admin instruction is demoted to a closing line.

### Added

* **Schedule board redesign.** The pinned decree calendar now renders a single bolded `Season ends` line at the top of the embed, partitions events into Active and Completed This Season blocks, and hides the Completed heading when no events have concluded yet. New `IScheduleField.isCompleted` boolean plus matching population in `ScheduleBoard.toField` drives the partition.
* **Decree editing UI.** Every post in the next-decree channel now carries an Edit button. Authorized users (server owner plus the guild's configured admin role) can adjust a decree's title, description, or fire time on a single occurrence (`Apply to this fire only`) or as a permanent shift to the recurring anchor (`Apply to all future fires`).
* **EventOverride model** with a compound unique index on `(eventId, originalOccurrence)`. Single-occurrence edits write here without mutating the underlying Event document. Read at fire time by `ReminderJob` and at render time by the next-decree post builder.
* **Timezone select dropdown.** Time edits surface a 25-option `StringSelectMenu` of curated IANA zones (UTC plus 24 high-traffic regional names) after modal submit. Discord modals cannot host select menus, so the timezone collection moved out of the modal into a follow-up ephemeral. The collector uses `createMessageComponentCollector` with explicit `ComponentType.StringSelect` to work around a discord.js 14.25 silent-drop when a select and a button share an ephemeral message.
* **Audit log.** Every edit writes a row to the `AuditLog` collection via `botLogStore.logAudit` with `actorId`, `eventId`, `guildId`, `action` (`decree_edit_once` or `decree_edit_permanent`), `before`, `after`, and `originalOccurrence`. Auditability is required for any feature that lets non-owners mutate scheduled events.
* **Boot announcement system content.** `embedContent.featureAnnouncement` rewritten for v1.4.0. Public embed in `#announcements` apologizes for the dissonance and walks through the four user-visible changes; admin embed in `#inner-sanctum` is the plain changelog plus the migration note. Idempotent via `botLogStore` keyed on `featureAnnouncedEvent("1.4.0")`.

### Changed

* **`parseModalValues` split** into `extractAndValidate` plus `resolveOverrideTime` so the structural validation can run before the timezone is collected, and the time resolution can run after. Original `parseModalValues` signature kept as a backward-compat wrapper for the unit tests that exercise the full pipeline.
* **`vitest.config.ts` resolve.alias** added explicitly. The bot's `tsconfig.json` excludes `**/*.test.ts`, and `vite-tsconfig-paths` honors that exclude; the result was that direct `@base/...` imports in test files failed to resolve while transitive imports through production files worked. The explicit alias map mirrors the tsconfig paths block and covers the test surface.
* **`package.json` test script** changed from a placeholder echo to `vitest run`. Added `test:watch` for development. Bumped version from 1.3.0 to 1.4.0, which keys the boot announcement.
* **devDependencies** gained `vitest`, `vite-tsconfig-paths`, `@vitest/coverage-v8`, `supertest`, and `@types/supertest`. None were in the lockfile despite the test files importing them.

### Migration required

After deploying this release, run the cadence migration once against the production MongoDB:

```
DRY_RUN=1 LIMIT_GUILD=<test-guild-id> node scripts/fix-event-cadences.mjs
LIMIT_GUILD=<test-guild-id> node scripts/fix-event-cadences.mjs
DRY_RUN=1 node scripts/fix-event-cadences.mjs
node scripts/fix-event-cadences.mjs
```

The script is idempotent: it filters by name plus the **old** `intervalHours` value (36 for Ruins, 84 for Altar), so re-running finds nothing to update. Without the migration, existing event documents keep their old `intervalHours` and the cadence drift continues for any guild that ran `/configure-kvk-season` before this release.

`scripts/fix-event-cadences.mjs` lives in `.gitignore` historically (the `scripts/*` line was added defensively), so it ships from local checkouts only, not from Railway. Run it from a developer machine pointed at the production `MONGOOSE_URI`.

### Tests added

* `occurrenceCalculator.test.ts` pins the JSON cadence values (Ruins 40, Altar 86, Kau Karuak 0) and reproduces four canonical schedule rows for both recurring events to verify cadence + calculator together produce the right dates.
* `ReminderScheduler.test.ts` covers the new guild-scoped dedup contract (one announcement per guild per season) and the cross-season replay case (a fresh `seasonEnd` opens a new dedup slot).
* `embedBuilder.test.ts` covers the schedule board redesign branches: bolded season-end line at top, active and completed partitioning, completed heading hidden when empty.
* `decreeEditHandlers.test.ts` covers the split helpers (`extractAndValidate`, `resolveOverrideTime`) directly plus the legacy `parseModalValues` wrapper for backward compat.
* `permissions.test.ts` covers `canEditDecree` (owner returns true, admin role match returns true, no role and not owner returns false).

### Known follow-ups (deferred to v1.4.1)

* `GuildSetupManager.test.ts` and `ChannelDeleteWatcher.test.ts` have 13 stale assertions that never accounted for the `nextDecreeChannelId` channel addition. Production code in those paths is exercised by every real guild boot and is verified working; the tests need fixture updates.
* `verifySignature.test.ts` has 4 assertion failures in the HMAC middleware tests. The middleware is only on the plugin-proxy path, which is gated behind `USE_REMOTE_EVENTS=true` (currently off in production). Not a release blocker; defer until the F4 cutover work resumes.
* Decree edit permission gate currently uses the single `adminRoleId` field on `GuildConfig`. The original Phase 4 design called for `authorizedRoleIds: string[]` on `GuildConfig` for multi-role authorization. If multi-role gating becomes a requested feature, widen the schema and migrate existing `adminRoleId` values into the array.
